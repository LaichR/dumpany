# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Streaming text output with configurable line width, indentation, and line endings.
- Structured, nested contexts with prefixes, prologues, and epilogues, with
  explicit recording destination selection and scoped target recording.
- Composition through iterators, joins, tuples, and the `Dump` trait.
- Explicit finalization and access to the underlying writer after finalization or failure.
